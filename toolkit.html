Safety Toolkit

Quick checklists you can use any time.

Spot a scam checklist

  • Does the message rush you to act?
  • Does it ask for money, passwords, or bank details?
  • Is the sender's address strange or misspelled?
  • Does it sound too good to be true?

Password tips

  • Use at least 12 characters.
  • Join three or four random words together.
  • Use a different password for each important account.
  • Never share a password by email or text.
