Is this news real?

Fake stories spread fast because they look real. Before you believe or share a story, follow these steps.

  1. Check the source. Do you know the website? Is there a real "About us" page?
  2. Look for the same story elsewhere. If only one site reports it, be careful.
  3. Check the date. Old stories are often shared as if they are new.

If a story makes you feel very angry or scared, stop and check it first. Fake stories are designed to do that.
